Aarav's Core Vulnerabilities & Actionable Fixes

Actionable Priorities
1. Trapped Elbow on Backhand Punch (#1 Recurring Flaw) Severity: Critical

Under quick drive pressure, Aarav pins his playing elbow against his right ribcage. With no forward hinge space, the paddle acts like a rigid flat board, dumping the ball into the net tape.

The 6-Inch Rule Fix:

Keep elbow anchored 6 inches in front of navel; snap forearm outward from 7 to 1 o'clock at bounce peak.

2. Post-Serve Heel-Lock & Crossover 3rd-Ball Jam Severity: High

Stands upright after serving with weight settling flat on heels. Deep receiver pushes directly into his playing elbow pocket catch him frozen between FH and BH decisions.

Zero-Second Split-Hop Fix:

Micro-hop into a 110° knee-flex stance before serve crosses net; elevate paddle tip to sternum height.

3. Loose Service & Half-Long Drift Severity: High

When rushed, thick paddle contact produces high-floating balls without spin that drift past the endline, giving opponents easy opening loop-kill setups.

Tangential Brush & Net Clearance Fix:

Strike lower at net-tape height with pure rubber friction. Practice keeping serves under a ribbon tied 2 inches above the net.

Open blade angle on fast counter-drives lacks topspin rotation, flying past baseline. On net-cord deflections, freezes and reaches with fingertips rather than stutter-stepping.

Magnus Arc & Micro-Stutter Fix:
