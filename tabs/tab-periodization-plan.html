Periodized 1-Month High-Performance Microcycle

Structured weekly progression balancing home solo drills, Nova S Pro robot settings, and club sparring with coach

4-Week Blueprint
PHASE 1 • DAYS 1–7 Posture Lockdown & Reset

Week 1: Stance Lockdown, Quad Endurance & Post-Serve Hop-Back Habit

Target: Eradicate post-serve freezing and standing tall. Build isometric leg endurance to hold 110° low squat throughout exchanges.

2.5 - 3 Hours Daily
Day 1 (Monday) Serve + Reset

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Agility ladder 2-in-2-out + 4x 45s wall-sit holds.

0:30-1:30 (Multi-ball / Nova): 150 Pendulum Serves. Rule: Instant hop-back into squat before ball hits opposite table. Video review 20 reps.

1:30-2:30 (Sparring): Practice matches; +1 bonus point every time a 3rd-ball counter is hit from low squat.

Day 2 (Tuesday) Forehand Arc

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Mini-band low squat lateral walks (3x20m) + Pallof press core anti-rotation.

0:30-1:30 (Nova S Pro): Slow heavy backspin to forehand. Closed bat (50°), brushing 6 o'clock to 12 o'clock for topspin curve.

1:30-2:30 (Coach / Club): Forehand topspin consistency; target 25 consecutive loop balls on table without flat slap.

Day 3 (Wednesday) BH Forward Elbow

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Agility ladder cadence drills + rotator cuff Y-T-W band raises.

0:30-1:30 (Multi-ball): Backhand block off rising ball. Anchor elbow 6 inches in front of navel; zero tucked elbow against ribs.

1:30-2:30 (Sparring): Conditioned game: Opponent attacks Vivaan's backhand; Vivaan punches down the line.

Day 4 (Thursday) Short Touch

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Right-foot forward lunge recovery drills (4x12 reps per leg) + mini-band hops.

0:30-1:30 (Robot / Multi): Step right foot under table; soft hands double-bounce drop push on short balls.

1:30-2:30 (Sparring): Short-game push duels. Winner must keep all pushes 2-bounce short or attack on 1st float.

Day 5 (Friday) 3rd-Ball Sequence

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Explosive medicine ball / band rotational twists + 4x 45s wall sits.

0:30-1:30 (Live Feeder): Serve short backspin → Partner pushes deep to forehand → Vivaan loops topspin down line.

1:30-2:30 (Match Sets): Best of 5 match against peer rival; audit 3rd ball conversion percentage.

Day 6 (Saturday) Falkenberg Drill

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Agility ladder in-and-out + lateral cone sprints (5 sets).

0:30-1:45 (Coach Multi-ball): Falkenberg footwork (BH from BH corner → FH from BH corner → FH wide).

1:45-2:45 (Sparring): Handicap match sets against senior club players (receive 2 points head start).

Day 7 (Sunday) Active Recovery & Video Self-Audit

0:00-0:45 (Regeneration): Full body mobility, hamstring/hip flexor foam rolling, light 15 min jog.

0:45-1:45 (Video Review & Tactical Study): Watch Coach Brad Han & Global TT tutorials on serve reset; compare with own match recordings.

1:45-2:15 (Solo Touch): 50 relaxed short ghost backspin serves on home table focusing on pure touch.

PHASE 2 • DAYS 8–14 Magnus Topspin Curve

Week 2: Eradicating Flat Slap & Developing Magnus Topspin Arc

Target: Replace flat 85° open paddle slapping with 50° closed upward brush. Create a 6-inch safety clearance over the net with diving baseline trajectory.

2.5 - 3 Hours Daily
Day 8 (Monday) 6 O'Clock Brush

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Diagonal resistance band woodchops (3x15) + wrist extensor conditioning.

0:30-1:30 (Shadow & Nova): 200 shadow loops (Brad Han "6 O'Clock" method). Nova S Pro low backspin: 50 consecutive loops with audible grazing brush.

1:30-2:30 (Sparring): Forehand topspin vs block rally. Count highest consecutive rally without missing long.

Day 9 (Tuesday) Heavy Backspin Lift

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Glute bridge holds + mini-band side shuffles (4x15m).

0:30-1:30 (Nova S Pro): Heavy chop balls. Focus on dropping paddle below table level before driving upward-forward.

1:30-2:30 (Coach Feeder): Multi-ball 3rd-ball loop against heavy backspin push; target deep corners.

Day 10 (Wednesday) Waist-Elbow Rule

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Medicine ball rotational chest passes + 4x 45s wall sits.

0:30-1:30 (Multi-ball): Brad Han Waist-Elbow synchronization. Elbow initiates loop forward, forearm snaps through contact.

1:30-2:30 (Sparring): No flat kill rule: Flat slaps concede point to opponent; only topspin winners count.

Day 11 (Thursday) Down-The-Line Loop

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Agility ladder in-and-out + single-leg hop balance holds.

0:30-1:30 (Live Feeder): Forehand loop from backhand corner down the line (Ti Long GoPro drill pattern).

1:30-2:30 (Sparring): Match sets targeting opponent's wide forehand with down-the-line loops.

Day 12 (Friday) Continuous Loop

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Core plank with arm raises + rotator cuff band pull-aparts.

0:30-1:30 (Nova S Pro): 2-ball sequence: Ball 1 heavy backspin (loop) → Ball 2 topspin block (continuous loop at 70% power).

1:30-2:30 (Sparring): 5-ball rally requirement before attacking kill shot allowed.

Day 13 (Saturday) Multi-ball Gauntlet

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Agility ladder + lateral shuttle sprints (6 sets).

0:30-1:45 (Coach Multi-ball): Rapid 50-ball crates: Random deep balls, testing topspin arc under cardio fatigue.

1:45-2:45 (Sparring): Match sets vs older defensive chop/block players.

Day 14 (Sunday) Recovery & Analysis

0:00-0:45 (Regeneration): Foam rolling lower back and glutes, light swim or walk.

0:45-1:45 (Video Audit): Review Week 2 sparring recordings; check blade closure angle at contact on 3rd balls.

1:45-2:15 (Serve Touch): 50 fast speed serves into body crossover corner.

PHASE 3 • DAYS 15–21 Front-to-Back Mobility

Week 3: In-and-Out Table Footwork & Backhand Elbow Forward Alignment

Target: Eliminate reaching waist-bends on short balls and cure the tucked-elbow habit. Master front-to-back transition and proactive banana flicks.

2.5 - 3 Hours Daily
Day 15 (Monday) In-and-Out Footwork

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Right foot deep forward lunge + pushback hop recovery (4x15 reps).

0:30-1:30 (Table Shadow & Nova): 100 shadow reps: Step in right foot, touch net post with bat, hop back into low squat to loop.

1:30-2:30 (Sparring): Conditioned game: Short receive drop → immediate pushback hop → loop 3rd ball.

Day 16 (Tuesday) BH Anchor Alignment

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Band internal/external shoulder rotations + thoracic extension on foam roller.

0:30-1:30 (Multi-ball Feeder): Fast drives to backhand. Elbow anchored 6 inches in front of navel; snap forearm outward from 7 to 1 o'clock.

1:30-2:30 (Coach Sparring): Coach fires rapid flat drives to Vivaan's elbow; Vivaan executes active punch blocks.

Day 17 (Wednesday) Banana Flick Tech

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Agility ladder lateral in-and-outs + medicine ball rotational slams.

0:30-1:30 (Nova S Pro): Half-long sidespin/under to backhand. Right foot steps in; wrist drops below ball and snaps banana flick down line.

1:30-2:30 (Sparring): Zero pushes allowed on half-long balls; every half-long ball must be flicked or looped.

Day 18 (Thursday) Transition Switching

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Mini-band low squat walks (4x20m) + 4x 45s wall-sit holds.

0:30-1:30 (Live Feeder): Brad Han Backhand-to-Forehand Transition: 1 BH block from center → 1 FH loop from wide corner.

1:30-2:30 (Sparring): Opponent plays alternate corners (BH corner → FH corner); focus on hip coil transition.

Day 19 (Friday) Skidding Chop-Push

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Rotator cuff deceleration Y-T-W raises + core plank variations.

0:30-1:30 (Nova S Pro): Deep backspin feeds: Practice fast skidding chop-push aimed at extreme white sideline.

1:30-2:30 (Match Sets): Best of 5 match against top junior rival; track return error percentages.

Day 20 (Saturday) Endurance Multi-ball

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Lateral cone shuffle push-offs (6x 10 touches) + agility ladder cadence.

0:30-1:45 (Coach Multi-ball): 1 short ball to net → 1 deep ball to wide FH corner → 1 fast drive to body hip.

1:45-2:45 (Sparring): Full handicap match play with senior club players.

Day 21 (Sunday) Recovery & Analysis

0:00-0:45 (Regeneration): Lower body static stretching, gentle jog, hydration & recovery.

0:45-1:45 (Video Audit): Audit Week 3 footage; compare backhand elbow spacing against Ti Long & Brad Han models.

1:45-2:15 (Serve Touch): 50 Reverse Pendulum serves using bottle cap drill methodology.

PHASE 4 • DAYS 22–28 Match Pressure & Tactical Conversion

Week 4: Match Set Simulation, 3rd-Ball Kill Chains & Competitive Composure

Target: Synthesize dynamic stance, elastic split-step, elbow forward alignment, and proactive 3rd/5th-ball kill shots under competitive tournament pressure.

2.5 - 3 Hours Daily
Day 22 (Monday) 3rd-Ball Pivot Loops

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Agility ladder forward-backwards hops + medicine ball rotational wall throw (3x15).

0:30-1:30 (Nova S Pro / Coach): Reverse Pendulum serve short to middle → return pushed long to BH → pivot step around and execute full-power FH loop down line (Ti Long Pivot Attack ↗).

1:30-2:30 (Sparring): Conditioned points: Vivaan serves only reverse/hook; must pivot-attack any return pushed past the endline.

Day 23 (Tuesday) Serve & Split-Hop Tempo

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Elastic pogo jumps (4x45s) + lateral resistance band shuffles (3x20m).

0:30-1:30 (Serve & Recover): 100 serves focusing strictly on the zero-sec hop back (Coach Brad Han 3rd-Ball Setups ↗). Coach catches or returns to random corner; Vivaan must be grounded before contact.

1:30-2:30 (Live Drill): Fast deep topspin serve → opponent blocks → Vivaan counters with mid-distance forehand drive.

Day 24 (Wednesday) Countering Half-Long Balls

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Deep lunge isometric holds (30s each leg) + thoracic rotation band pulls.

0:30-1:30 (Nova S Pro Feeder): Half-long balls with variable spin. Vivaan steps right foot under table to open-face loop or chiquita flick (Ti Long Half-Long Attacks ↗); zero tentative pushes permitted.

1:30-2:30 (Sparring): Best of 5 game where pushing a half-long ball counts as an automatic concession point.

Day 25 (Thursday) Multi-Ball High Tempo

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Cone shuttle sprint & touch (8 reps) + rotator cuff deceleration Y-T-W raises.

0:30-1:30 (Rapid Multi-ball): 5-ball high tempo sequence: 1 short drop → 1 deep BH → 1 mid-table drive → 1 wide FH loop → 1 smash kill (Ti Long WTT Drills ↗). 10 full sets.

1:30-2:30 (Sparring): High-pressure sets starting at score 8-8 to train tactical decision-making under duress.

Day 26 (Friday) Tournament Simulation

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Dynamic warm-up, split-step rhythm activation, gentle shoulder bands.

0:30-2:15 (Club Tournament Matches): 3 consecutive Best of 5 match sets against distinct playstyles (1 pips/chopper or blocker, 1 aggressive looper, 1 senior veteran).

2:15-2:45 (Father / Coach Review): Tally unforced errors vs forced winners; check knee bend consistency on video.

Day 27 (Saturday) Serve Deception & Master Finishes

0:00-0:30 (Physical): Core hollow holds + jump rope double-unders + dynamic stretching.

0:30-1:30 (Serve Lab): Practice identical fake follow-through on heavy backspin vs dead knuckleball serves (Global TT 2-Layer Deception ↗ / Heavy Spin Master ↗).

1:30-2:30 (Competitive Match): Full handicap match play. Target: Win at least 4 direct service points per game.

Day 28 (Sunday) Cycle Milestone Audit & Regeneration

0:00-0:45 (Regeneration & Mobility): Full body foam rolling, hamstring/hip flexor stretches, breathing and recovery protocols.

0:45-1:45 (Microcycle Video Comparison): Record 30 forehand loops and 30 backhand drives on high-speed camera. Compare side-by-side with Day 1 match footage to quantify knee flexion angle, elbow clearance, and recovery speed.

1:45-2:30 (Goal Calibration): Review monthly metrics with father. Set goals for upcoming district/state tournament schedule.

Father's Sideline Action Rule

Do not give him multiple mechanical tips at once. Pick ONE cue per session: e.g., "Stay in the low squat" or "Hop back right after your serve". When he does that one thing right, praise him immediately. Developing these fundamental habits locally will make him a top-16 national caliber player without putting financial strain on your family.
