Father's Sideline Action Rule & Matchday Psychology for Aarav

Golden Sideline Principle
1. The "Single Cue" Rule

Never give multiple mechanical tips at once. Pick ONE clear cue per match (e.g. "Stay in the low squat" or "Hop back right after your serve"). When he executes that cue, give immediate fist-pump praise.

2. Praise Recovery, Not Just Points

Cheer loudly when Aarav does an elastic split-hop or holds his 6-inch backhand elbow pocket, even if the ball goes out. Rewarding the biomechanical habit builds autonomous national-caliber reflex memory.

3. Emotional Anchor at 8-8

When the set reaches 8-8 or deuce, remind him to take full 5 seconds before serving. Walk back, tap the table, wipe his blade, and commit 100% to his signature stealth or hook serve without rushing.
