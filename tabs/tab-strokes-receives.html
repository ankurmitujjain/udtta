Short Underspin / No-Spin to Forehand Short Length Reaching Waist Poke: Feet stayed behind baseline; bent at waist with overextended arm. Produced a floating push that landed mid-table. Right-Foot Step-In Short Drop (Stop Push): Step right foot deep under table with soft hands; contact ball off the bounce to keep it short (2 bounces).

Stepping in stabilizes his center of gravity directly behind the paddle. A short drop-push forces the opponent to reach in, killing all angles for a third-ball attack.

Half-Long Sidespin / Topspin to Backhand Drifting Off Edge Passive Center Push / Tucked Elbow: Absorbed the ball passively with elbow trapped against his ribs, sending a slow ball into the opponent's forehand zone. Early Backhand Banana Flick / Drive: Step in with elbow anchored forward; snap wrist around the ball to accelerate down the line or deep cross-court.

Half-long balls should never be pushed. The banana flick turns defense into offense immediately, forcing the server to react rather than dictating the rally.

Instant Backward Split-Step & Opening Loop: Hop backward 6 inches to create hitting distance; brush the ball at peak bounce into the opponent's playing elbow.

Taking a split-step back clears the hip, turning a defensive jamming ball into a clean forehand attack zone. Brushing creates a high safety arc over the net.
